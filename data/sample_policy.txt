Acme Corp Remote Work Policy

1. Eligibility
Full-time employees who have completed 90 days of employment are eligible to
work remotely up to three days per week. Contractors are not covered by this
policy and should refer to their statement of work.

2. Equipment
Acme provides each remote employee with a laptop, an external monitor, and a
one-time home-office stipend of 500 USD. Employees are responsible for a
reliable internet connection of at least 50 Mbps.

3. Security
Remote workers must connect through the company VPN when accessing internal
systems. Devices must have full-disk encryption enabled and automatic screen
lock set to five minutes. Storing customer data on personal devices is
strictly prohibited.

4. Working Hours
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local time zone.
Outside of core hours, employees may set their own schedule as long as weekly
deliverables are met.

5. Reimbursement
Approved co-working space memberships are reimbursed up to 200 USD per month.
Receipts must be submitted within 30 days through the expense portal.

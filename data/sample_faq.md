# Acme Product FAQ

## Billing

**How does billing work?**
Acme bills monthly on the first of each month. Plans are prorated when you
upgrade mid-cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing
period.

**Do you offer refunds?**
We offer a full refund within 14 days of the initial purchase. After 14 days,
charges are non-refundable but you can cancel to stop future billing.

## Data & Privacy

**Where is my data stored?**
Customer data is stored in encrypted form in US-East and EU-West regions.
You can choose your data residency region during onboarding.

**How long is data retained after cancellation?**
After cancellation we retain your data for 30 days to allow reactivation,
after which it is permanently deleted.

## Support

**What are your support hours?**
Standard support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 US Eastern.
Enterprise plans include 24/7 priority support with a one-hour response SLA.
